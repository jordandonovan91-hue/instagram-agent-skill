1  COVER  YOUR WEEKLY AI ROUTINE
    15 minutes a day. Monday to Friday.
2  STAKE  You don't need more apps.
    You need the same 5 small habits each week.
3  MONDAY  PLAN THE WEEK
    "Here's my week: [jobs, meetings]. Put it in order and flag anything that clashes."
4  TUESDAY  CLEAR THE INBOX
    "Sort these emails into reply today, this week, ignore: [subject lines]."
5  WEDNESDAY  CHASE THE MONEY
    "Write a polite reminder for an unpaid invoice: [number, amount, due date]."
6  THURSDAY  ONE POST
    "Write a short post from this customer question: [question]. Under 60 words."
7  FRIDAY  LOOK BACK
    "Here's what happened this week: [notes]. What went well, what didn't, and one thing to change."
8  WEEKEND  OFF
    No AI. No inbox. That's the point of the other five days.
9  TIP  SAVE THEM ONCE
    Put all five in your notes app so they're one copy and paste away.
10  RECAP  Mon plan · Tue inbox · Wed money · Thu post · Fri look back
    Save this and start Monday.
