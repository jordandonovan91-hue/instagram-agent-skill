1  COVER  7 PROMPTS FOR QUOTING FASTER
    Number 5 gets you paid sooner.
2  STAKE  The fastest quote usually wins the job.
    Not the cheapest. The fastest.
3  PROMPT 1  VOICE MEMO TO QUOTE
    "Turn this voice memo transcript into a tidy quote for my customer: [paste]. List the work, materials and total. Mark anything unclear with [check]."
4  PROMPT 2  THE SCOPE LINE
    "Write one clear sentence saying what this quote covers and what it doesn't: [job]."
5  PROMPT 3  THE OPTIONS QUOTE
    "Turn this job into 3 options, good, better and best, with what each one includes: [job]."
6  PROMPT 4  THE COVER EMAIL
    "Write a short email to send with this quote. Thank them, give the total, and say how long it's valid: [details]."
7  PROMPT 5  GET PAID SOONER
    "Write the payment terms for my quote: [deposit], balance due [when], how to pay. Clear and friendly, under 50 words."
8  PROMPT 6  THE FOLLOW-UP
    "Write a friendly 3 day follow-up text for a quote I sent [customer first name]. One question, no pressure."
9  PROMPT 7  THE PRICE QUESTION
    "A customer says my quote is too high. Write a calm reply that explains the value and offers one cheaper option: [details]."
10  RECAP  1. Voice memo  2. Scope line  3. Options  4. Cover email  5. Payment terms  6. Follow-up  7. Price question
    Check every number before you send. Save this for your next quote.
