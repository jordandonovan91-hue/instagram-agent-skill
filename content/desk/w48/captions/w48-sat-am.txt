ChatGPT or Claude? Short answer: both have free versions and both are good.

My rough rule:
ChatGPT for images, voice chats and quick answers.
Claude for long documents and writing that sounds more human.

Best test: paste the same prompt into both, like "Try the same prompt in both: Write a friendly reply to this customer: [message]. Under 50 words.", and keep the one you like.

Features change often, so check what's in the free plan when you sign up.
