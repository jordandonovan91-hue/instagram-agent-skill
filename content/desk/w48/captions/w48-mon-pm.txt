Answering the same DMs every night? Save the answers once.

Paste this into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini:

"Write 5 short saved replies for my [type of business] Instagram DMs: price question, opening hours, how to book, where we work, and 'are you available'. Friendly, under 40 words each."

Then add them in Instagram under Settings > Business tools > Saved replies, each with a short shortcut. (Menu names move around, so search "saved replies" if you can't find it.)
