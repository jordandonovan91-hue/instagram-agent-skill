Hiring? Don't start from a blank page.

Paste this into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini:

"Write a short, friendly job ad for a [role] at my [type of business] in [suburb]. Hours: [hours]. Pay: [pay or award]. What the day looks like, who'd love it, how to apply. Under 150 words, no buzzwords."

Before you post it, check the pay against the right award on the Fair Work website. AI can't do that bit for you.
