Hot take: asking AI for "ideas" just gives you more work. Ask it for a checklist.

Paste this into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini:

"Make me a step-by-step checklist for [job, like onboarding a new customer]. Max 10 steps, in order, one line each, with who does it."

Works for onboarding a customer, prepping a job, or handing work to staff. Print it and tick it off.
